## NOTE ON READ-ONLY vs WRITABLE DISTINCTION
## ──────────────────────────────────────────
## public.gold_open_shortfalls  ← READ-ONLY synced table (managed by Lakebase sync pipeline;
##                                 is a Partitioned table; only SELECT/CREATE INDEX allowed;
##                                 modifications would break the sync)
##
## northpeak.replenishment_actions  ← WRITABLE operational table (regular heap table with
##                                     sequence PK, application DML, FK references, CHECKs)
## northpeak.action_audit           ← WRITABLE operational table (regular heap table with
##                                     sequence PK and FK back to replenishment_actions)
##
## The writable tables live in schema 'northpeak' and were created by the app's Drizzle
## migration. The synced table lives in schema 'public' and is managed by the Lakebase
## reverse-ETL pipeline. They are distinct by schema, table type (heap vs partitioned),
## and mutability constraints.
